Format rules

Standalone house-format restrictions

This list completely replaces the official Advanced Format Forbidden & Limited List for events using this house format.

  • Forbidden: 0 copies.
  • Limited: up to 1 copy.
  • Semi-Limited: up to 2 copies.
  • Unlisted and within the JUSH card pool: up to 3 copies.

Card pool: TCG cards first released no later than 1 August 2025 (including all of Justice Hunters/JUSH). Later cards are not legal, even if not listed above. Reprints after the cutoff do not exclude cards released earlier.

House tweaks: Cards marked NEW differ from the official JUSH-era banlist. The marker compares against that fixed baseline, not against the previous house update.

Limits apply across the Main Deck, Extra Deck, and Side Deck combined.
