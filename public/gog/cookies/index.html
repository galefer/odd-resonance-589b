Cookies and device storage

Signing in

JUSH uses the first-party jush_member cookie to recognise your player session. It contains a random session token. Your password manager can remember your password when you choose Save in your browser.

A regular session has a 12-hour server limit. Choosing “Stay signed in on this device” keeps your session for up to 30 days. Sign out in your player account to end it. On a shared phone, leave that box unticked.

Staff access uses a separate gog_session cookie. Both session cookies are restricted to secure connections.

Decks and preferences

Builder saves decks and preferences in this browser using IndexedDB and browser storage. The event page temporarily remembers the last loaded schedule. For up to five minutes, this tab remembers your public account icon, display name and the last published card list. It checks for updates when you switch screens. The installed app caches its navigation and an offline page. Clearing site data removes local decks. Export decks you want to keep first.

Adding a favourite to your profile publishes a copy of its card list. Visitors can view it or save their own copy. Removing the favourite retires its share link.

Your player profile

Your handle, display name, chosen artwork, favourite decks and rated standings are visible to visitors. Your optional real name is private unless you choose to share it with signed-in players. The event director can review match records, correct results and help with account access. Contact the JUSH director at Guild of Games for account or privacy enquiries.
