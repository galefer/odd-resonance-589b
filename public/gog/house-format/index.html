Guild of Games house rules

Justice Hunters card pool. One altered banlist.

The Guild of Games house format is based on the JUSH-era banlist, with its own published changes. It fully replaces the standard Advanced Format list. A card is eligible if it was released in any TCG region, including North America, on or before 1 August 2025. Battles of Legend: Monster Mayhem (BLMM) qualifies through its 13 June 2025 North American release, even where it arrived later. Later reprints of eligible cards remain eligible; an OCG-only release does not qualify. Eligible cards are only restricted when they appear here. Main Deck, Extra Deck, and Side Deck copies are counted together.
